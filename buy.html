Our 40-point check

We'd rather lose a sale than sell you a dud

A used phone that fails in a fortnight costs us far more in reputation than it ever made us at the till. So everything gets the same check, and anything that doesn't pass either gets fixed or doesn't go out.

If a device has a quirk we can't fix — a faint screen mark, a battery that's seen better days — we tell you before you buy and price it accordingly. You'll never find out afterwards.

After you buy

Ninety days, in writing

The difference between a shop and a stranger is what happens when something goes wrong.

90-day warranty

Covers faults with the device itself. Something goes wrong in that window, bring it back and we put it right — no argument, no restocking fee.

Set up before you leave

We'll move your photos, contacts, messages and apps across from your old device and check everything works before you walk out.

We're not going anywhere

A shopfront on Rideau Street with our name on it. No blocked number, no deleted listing, no meeting in a parking lot.

The honest caveat: the warranty covers faults, not accidents. Drop it, crack it or get it wet after you've bought it and that's a separate conversation — though we'll always give you a straight price for putting it right.
