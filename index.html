Questions

Everything people ask at the counter

How much will you pay for my phone?
It depends on the model, the storage size, the battery health, the condition and whether it's carrier-locked. Call with those details and we'll give you a realistic range in about a minute — then confirm the exact figure once we've tested it in front of you. The valuation is free and you're never under any obligation to sell.
Do you buy broken or cracked phones?
Yes. Cracked screens, dead batteries, water damage and devices that won't power on are all things we buy. We price the repair into the offer and tell you exactly how we got to the number, so you're not guessing why a damaged device is worth less.
What do I need to bring to sell a device?
Three things. Sign out of iCloud or your Google account first — we can't legally resell a device still locked to you. Bring photo ID, which Ontario law requires us to record for second-hand purchases. And bring the charger and box if you still have them; they nudge the value up, but they're not essential.
How do I get paid?
Cash on the spot, or store credit toward something else on the shelf — your choice, the moment you accept the offer. No waiting for a transfer to clear and no mail-in envelope.
Will my personal data be wiped?
Yes, and we do it in front of you. Every device we take in is factory reset at the counter before it goes anywhere near the shelf. If you want your photos and contacts moved onto a new device first, we'll do that for you as part of the sale.
Are the phones you sell unlocked, and do they come with a warranty?
We unlock wherever the model and carrier allow, and we tell you upfront when a device is locked to a particular network. Everything we sell carries a 90-day written warranty covering faults — we don't sell "as is, no returns". Accidental damage after you buy isn't covered.
Can I trade my old phone against a newer one?
That's usually the best-value option. We value your old device, put it straight against whatever you're upgrading to, and you pay only the difference — which is sometimes nothing at all. We'll transfer your photos, contacts, messages and apps before you leave.
Where exactly are you, and is there parking?
We're at 308 Rideau Street, Unit A, a short walk east of the ByWard Market and steps from the Rideau transit station. There's metered street parking on Rideau and paid lots nearby.
