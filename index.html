Questions

Everything people ask at the counter

How long does a phone screen repair take?
Most screen replacements are finished in 30 to 60 minutes while you wait, as long as we have your model's part in stock. Call ahead with your exact model and we'll confirm stock and set the part aside for you.
Do I need an appointment?
No — walk in any day we're open. Calling first is still worth it at busy times so we can check the part is on the shelf and give you a realistic wait.
Will I lose my photos and data during a repair?
Standard repairs like screens, batteries and charging ports don't touch your storage, so your data stays exactly as it was. We still recommend backing up before any repair, and for water damage or a device that won't power on we'll always talk you through the risk first. We never browse your device, and nothing is copied without your permission.
Do you repair water-damaged phones?
Yes, and the sooner the better. Power the device off, don't charge it, and bring it straight in — rice does nothing but waste hours. We open it up, ultrasonically clean the board and treat the corrosion. The diagnostic is free, and we tell you honestly whether it's worth repairing.
Is there a warranty on repairs?
Every repair is covered for 90 days on both the part and the workmanship. If something related to our work goes wrong in that window, bring it back and we'll put it right at no charge. Accidental damage and liquid exposure after the repair aren't covered.
Do you really beat other quotes?
Show us a written quote from another Ottawa shop for the same repair with comparable parts and we'll beat it. That's the whole idea behind "unbeatable prices" — we'd rather earn your business than watch you walk down the street.
Will you buy a phone that's broken or locked?
We buy phones, tablets and laptops in almost any condition, working or not, and price them accordingly. The one thing we can't take is a device still locked to someone else's account — please remove iCloud or Google account locks and bring proof of ownership.
Where exactly are you, and is there parking?
We're at 308 Rideau Street, Unit A, a short walk east of the ByWard Market and steps from the Rideau transit station. There's metered street parking on Rideau and paid lots nearby.
