Questions

What people ask about laptops

How much will you pay for my laptop?
It depends on the model and year, the processor, the RAM and storage, the battery's health and cycle count, and the condition of the screen, keyboard and case. Call with those details and we'll give you a realistic range in about a minute — then confirm the exact figure once we've tested it in front of you. The valuation is free and you're never under any obligation to sell.
How do I find my laptop's model and specs?
On a Mac, open the Apple menu and choose About This Mac. On Windows, go to Settings, then System, then About. Between that screen and the model number printed on the underside, you'll have everything we need to price it. If it won't turn on, the underside alone is enough to get us started.
Do you buy laptops that don't work?
Yes. Cracked screens, dead batteries, sticky or missing keys, liquid spills and laptops that won't power on are all things we buy. We factor the condition into the offer and tell you exactly how we got to the number, so you're not guessing why a faulty laptop is worth less.
What do I need to bring to sell a laptop?
Three things. Unlock it first: on a Mac, turn off Find My and sign out of your Apple ID; on any laptop, remove a firmware or BIOS password if you set one. Bring photo ID, which we're required to record for second-hand purchases. And bring the charger if you still have it — it makes testing quicker and nudges the value up.
Do you buy work or school laptops?
Yes, as long as it's yours to sell and it's been released. A laptop still enrolled in an employer's or school's device management can't be reset for resale, so ask their IT team to remove it before you come in.
Will my files be wiped?
Yes, and we do it in front of you. Every laptop we take in is wiped at the counter before it goes anywhere near the shelf, so back up anything you want to keep before you come in. Trading up with us? We'll move your files across to the new laptop as part of the sale.
Do your laptops come with a warranty?
Yes. Every laptop we sell carries a 90-day written warranty covering faults, and we tell you the battery's health and cycle count before you pay, so there are no surprises. Accidental damage after you buy, like drops and spills, isn't covered.
Can I trade my old laptop against a newer one?
That's usually the best-value option. We value your old laptop, put it straight against whatever you're upgrading to, and you pay only the difference — which is sometimes nothing at all. An old phone or tablet can count toward it too.
