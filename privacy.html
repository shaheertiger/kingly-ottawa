Privacy & cookies

This page explains what happens to your information when you use this website — what's collected, who sees it, and how to opt out.

We're King Mobile + Laptop Experts, 308 Rideau St, Unit A, Ottawa, ON K1N 5Y4. You can reach us on (613) 206-6060 about anything on this page.

The short version

This site has no forms, no accounts and no shopping cart, so there's nothing here for you to fill in and nothing for us to store about you directly. What we do run is Google's advertising tag, which uses cookies to tell us whether our ads are working. You can turn that off — see Your choices.

What this website collects

  • Nothing you type. There are no contact or enquiry forms. If you want to reach us, you call or walk in.
  • Standard server logs. Our host records the usual technical details of a request — IP address, browser, the page asked for, the time. This is ordinary web-server housekeeping used to keep the site running and secure.
  • Advertising and measurement cookies, described next.

Advertising measurement

We advertise on Google. To find out whether those ads actually bring people in — rather than guessing — the site loads the Google Ads tag (gtag.js). It sets cookies that let Google tell us things like "someone who clicked our ad later tapped the phone number", without telling us who you are.

Specifically, the tag records when a visitor:

  • taps one of our phone numbers, and
  • asks for directions to the shop.

Google may also use these cookies to show you our ads again elsewhere (remarketing) and to measure ad performance in aggregate. Google acts as our data processor for measurement and as an independent controller for its own advertising purposes. What Google does with the data is covered by Google's privacy policy and Google's business data terms.

Enhanced conversions

We have Google's enhanced conversions feature switched on. Where a customer has given us contact details for this purpose, the tag converts those details into an irreversible cryptographic hash in your browser and sends only the hash to Google, so it can match the visit to an ad click. The original details are never transmitted.

Today there is nowhere on this site to enter such details, so in practice nothing is being sent. If that changes — for example if we add a booking form — this page will be updated first, and we'll only send details given to us for that purpose.

Google Maps

The map showing our location is embedded from Google Maps. Loading it means your browser contacts Google, which may set its own cookies, exactly as if you had opened Google Maps yourself.

Fonts

Our typeface is served by Google Fonts, so your browser requests the font files from Google. No cookies are set for this.

Your choices

  • Ad personalisation. Turn it off for your Google account at My Ad Center.
  • Block the cookies. Every major browser lets you block or clear third-party cookies for a site. The website works perfectly well with them blocked — we just lose the measurement.
  • Browser tracking protection. Built-in protections in Safari, Firefox and Brave, or an ad blocker, will stop the tag loading. We don't work around that.
  • Ask us. Call (613) 206-6060 with any question about this page, or to ask what we hold about you.

Changes to this page

If we add anything that collects information — a form, a booking system, a chat widget — we'll update this page before it goes live.
