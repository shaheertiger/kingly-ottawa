Repairs for phones, tablets & laptops

Every repair below is done in-shop at 308 Rideau Street by technicians who do this all day. Most are finished while you wait, and every one is tested in front of you before you pay.

Free quote: (613) 206-6060 Jump to price list

Screen replacement

The repair we do most. Whether it's a hairline crack or a screen that's gone black entirely, we replace the assembly and colour-match the display so it looks and responds like the original.

  • Cracked or shattered glass
  • Black, flickering or lined display
  • Touch that won't respond, or "ghost touch"
  • True Tone and Face ID preserved where the model allows

from $79   30–60 min

Battery replacement

Phone batteries wear out after a few hundred charge cycles — that's normal, not a reason to buy a new phone. A fresh high-capacity cell usually costs a fraction of an upgrade.

  • Dies by mid-afternoon on a full charge
  • Shuts off suddenly at 20–40%
  • Swollen battery lifting the screen (bring it in now — this is a fire risk)
  • Phone runs hot or throttles under light use

from $49   30–45 min

Charging port repair

Before we replace anything, we check the obvious: years of pocket lint compacted into the port. Often that's a quick clean-out rather than a repair — and we'll tell you so rather than charge you for parts.

  • Only charges when the cable is held at an angle
  • Charges slowly, or stops and starts
  • Cable feels loose or falls out
  • No charge at all from any cable or brick

from $59   45–90 min

Water & liquid damage

Time matters more than anything else here. Corrosion starts within hours, so the sooner it reaches our bench the better the odds.

Right now, before you come in: power it off, don't plug it in, and don't put it in rice — rice does nothing except delay the repair.

  • Full teardown and ultrasonic board cleaning
  • Corrosion treatment and component-level inspection
  • Honest verdict: we'll say if it isn't worth saving
  • Data recovery attempted even on boards that can't be revived

free diagnostic   24–48 hrs

Camera, speaker & microphone

The parts that quietly stop working. If people can't hear you on calls or every photo comes out hazy, it's usually a module swap rather than anything serious.

  • Cracked camera lens glass
  • Blurry, hazy or unfocusable photos
  • Callers can't hear you, or you can't hear them
  • Distorted, crackling or silent loudspeaker

from $49   45–90 min

Back glass & housing

Shattered rear glass isn't just cosmetic — it lets dust and moisture into the chassis and it'll cut your fingers. We remove the old glass with a laser-free process that protects the wireless charging coil.

  • Cracked or missing back glass
  • Bent or dented frames
  • Worn power, volume or mute buttons
  • Wireless charging restored and tested

from $69   1–2 hrs

Laptop & MacBook repair

Windows or macOS, we take both. From a cracked lid to a machine that takes four minutes to reach the desktop, most laptops are worth repairing long before they're worth replacing.

  • Cracked or dim screens and failed hinges
  • Keyboards, trackpads and worn batteries
  • Liquid spills and overheating or noisy fans
  • SSD upgrades, RAM upgrades and clean OS installs

from $79   1–3 days

Data recovery & transfer

The photos matter more than the phone. Even when a device won't power on, there's often a path to the storage — and we'll try it before you give up.

  • Photos, videos, contacts and messages off dead devices
  • Full transfer when you upgrade — apps, accounts and all
  • Recovery from failed hard drives and SSDs
  • Your data is never copied, kept or viewed beyond the job

from $59   varies
