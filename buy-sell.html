Bring these with you

Three things get you the best price and the fastest turnaround:

  1. Sign out of iCloud or your Google account

    A device still locked to your account can't legally be resold, so we can't buy it until the lock is removed. We'll help you do it at the counter if you know your password.

  2. Photo ID

    Ontario law requires us to record ID for second-hand device purchases. It protects you as much as us — it's how stolen devices get traced.

  3. The charger and box, if you still have them

    Not essential, but originals nudge the value up. No box is no problem.

Straight talk

What actually moves the price

No mystery formula. These are the four things we look at, in order.

Model & storage

The single biggest factor. A higher-storage model of the same phone can be worth noticeably more.

Battery health

We read the actual cycle count and capacity rather than guessing. A tired battery costs us a part, so it costs a little off the offer.

Screen & body condition

Scratches are fine. Cracks mean we're fitting a new screen before resale, and the offer reflects exactly that cost.

Locks & carrier status

Unlocked devices are worth more. Account locks (iCloud, Google) must be removed before we can buy at all.

Every device we sell has been through this

  • Full function test — screen, cameras, speakers, mics, buttons, every sensor
  • Battery health measured and disclosed, not hidden
  • Any failing part replaced before it hits the shelf
  • Factory reset and checked against stolen-device registries
  • Carrier-unlocked wherever the model and carrier allow
  • Sold with a written warranty — never "as is, no returns"
